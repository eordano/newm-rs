# NewmCanvas: what is proven and what to fix

newm-canvas's tiled-window decisions: rectangles on an integer tile grid, placement by the engine's layout (a free choice that place checks: targets, spans, no overlap, no island split) with the push as fallback on grow, a minimum's growth, move and swap, the lake/ocean fill on shrink and close unless it splits an island, the gather after a bridge closes or moves and gather_islands, which gathers every island, undo and redo, minimum spans, the zoom as a camera, and the view's least-movement rule, for every public operation (add, add_at, remove, resize, swap, send_to_edge, move_to, move_by, zoom_by, set_min, focus, set_screen, set_config, rename, undo, redo, gather_islands), and the recency list that picks the focus after a close. It also states the span principle: a window's span changes only through a resize or its minimum, whatever moves it (C47), and a tile that leaves its canvas carries its span to the next (C48); the niri glue that keeps a drag on its canvas and carries the span (src/layout/canvas/moves.rs) is anchored. It also states that the faster placement search reads the same values: the range tree over top-left corners (src/index.rs) returns exactly the rectangles a scan finds (overlapping, touching, disjoint), its flood numbers islands so splits, is_bridge and the search's leaf checks (the flood at the root, union-find and mending per step) answer Canvas.splits and Canvas.is_bridge, and the search's tile term from shared areas counts the tiles the scan counted, summing to rule 1's tiles reassigned in any placement order; the search step's candidate grid (difference arrays) finds the blocked, clear and pending candidates the scans found, and its room-local cost is fresh_term. Floating windows, rendering, animation and input are outside the model.

## Not covered

- Floating point: tile size, need, fits (fits_at_1to1 at zoom 0, fits_on_screen at the current zoom), open_span, least and is_visible are modeled over exact integers in 1/10000 px with need and EPS as parameters (need >= 1, EPS >= 0); f64 rounding is not modeled. Config::default's 0.8 step is exact in that unit for zoom -2..4; above 4 the model's tile is 0, and no example zooms there.
- i32 tile coordinates and i64 physical pixels are modeled over Z: overflow is out of the model.
- Which window receives focus through focus_dir, focus_view_center, or neighbour as the swap partner is a free choice in the model; every claim holds for any choice. After a close it is the head of recent (C31); nearest is reached only when no window is left.
- scroll_by and set_view move the view without showing the focus; C10 excludes them (a pan is followed by focus_view_center).
- C10 still states its rename case under focus_known (the focus is a window), as before; C17 and C30 now prove it on every trace (recency_held), so it no longer needs check().
- The view rescale in zoom_by (centred_view, about the middle of the screen) is modeled with floored integer division in 1/10000 px: C38 keeps the middle to within that unit, and C39's round trip needs the rescale to land on it, which f64 rounding does not model; C22 and C40 bind exact cases, and C36 and C11 hold from any rescaled view. Coming closer and pulling back again returns only when coming closer does not scroll; when it scrolls by s, the view ends s * t0 / t1 off (tests/replay.rs a_zoom_keeps_the_middle_of_the_screen shows one).
- chain's queue is modeled with n+1 pops, enough when ids are unique (C17 keeps them unique); its content is used only by C07 and C08, as the code's own condition.
- Floating windows, rendering, animation and input.
- That recent lists ids in the order they were last focused is read from set_focus (retain, then insert at 0) and replayed by C34; the claims state that it is a permutation with the focus first (C30) and what a close picks (C31).
- spot is modeled exactly (gap with the corner rule, least scroll at the current zoom, side, distance, i then j, strict improvement). Rust's loop over d is unbounded; the model stops after far + 2 rounds and falls back to the spot right of the focus past every right edge, which is free and has gap at most far + 1, so the search finds a spot by then; that the fallback is never taken is argued here, not proven.
- need is a parameter of the config, not computed from base_tile in the model; C21, C22 and C37 bind Config::default's and CONFIGS[3]'s need at their 1:1 tiles. The model's tile at zoom 0 is base_tile because scale() is 1 there.
- The niri glue draws the zoom through a camera about the canvas origin and makes the view follow its animated scale so each frame keeps the middle (src/layout/canvas.rs view_point_with); that is outside the model and checked by src/layout/tests/canvas.rs canvas_zoom_animates_about_the_middle_of_the_screen.
- Which free spot add_at picks when at's is taken, beyond C46's replayed sequences: the claims state that it is free (C45), at the open span (C44), and that nothing moves (C43), not that no earlier spot in spot_near's ring order is free.
- The glue in src/layout/canvas/moves.rs is bound by anchor and hash only: the model does not run it, and this gate's checks cannot build the niri crate. Its tests run with the niri crate's tests: src/layout/tests/canvas.rs canvas_drag_keeps_the_span, canvas_drop_on_its_own_tiles_changes_nothing, canvas_window_keeps_its_span_on_another_workspace, and check_spans_kept after every operation of canvas_random_operations, which exempts only the explicit resizes and swap.
- A drag that leaves the window's workspace (another output, or another workspace in the overview) still takes the tile off its canvas at that moment, so its lake closes then, not at the drop; the span it carries is kept (C48).
- A tile keeps carrying its span through a floating spell, so a window tiled again reopens at the span it last held on a canvas.
- Where a swap leaves the two windows: the replay harness checks after every random swap that both land exactly on Canvas::swapped's rectangles, and the fixed test checks a swap and its way back; the model proves only that both keep their spans (C47) and that nothing overlaps (C01), not that the second push leaves the focus where the first put it.
- Rule 1 (the least cost: tiles reassigned, distance and diagonal weights) and rule 3 (a diagonal move charged) are the engine's cost; the model takes the engine's layout as a free choice and proves what place checks (C50), while tests/rules.rs's exact search checks optimality.
- gather's moves are a free choice that the model keeps only under gather's check (spans kept, no overlap); its own loop already breaks at the first window in its way, so no mutant of that check is declared.
- Undo and redo history (the stacks, HISTORY, forget_history on add, add_at, remove and rename) is not modeled: Restore takes any snapshot, and the claims hold for every one.
- The validation mutants of place and restore are killed by direct tests (place_accepts_only_layouts_that_keep_the_rules, restore_refuses_a_partial_overlapping_or_empty_snapshot): the engine itself never returns a layout they would refuse.
- Complexity is argued and measured, not proven: a range-tree query visits at most one node per level that straddles each end of the column range (O(log² n + k)), the cost's tile term is O(m²) shared areas for m moved windows, and the leaf's flood is O(n log² n). newm-canvas/tests/bench.rs measures it: at 119x113 tiles with 64 windows a seeded run takes about 0.1 s instead of 4.8 s.
- Search::run's control flow (the candidate order, pruning against the best, the budget) is not modeled: arrange stays a free choice that place checks (C50). The claims show that every value it reads from the new structures is the value the scan computed: conflicts and the windows under a candidate come from the tree over the layout before, where a window not yet placed still lies (an invariant of run, read from the code), C53; the blocked, clear and pending candidates from the candidate grid, C64-C69; kept tiles, C56; the leaf's island check, C61; the tile term per moved window, C62. place::tests::arrange_decides_what_the_scan_decided compares every placement against place::reference (the old search, kept under cfg(test)), and tests/bench.rs's layout digests match the old code's.
- Node::corners reads a whole bin with std's partition_point (modeled as dropWhile on its sorted rows, equal on a sorted list) and the sorts are std's sort_unstable (modeled by Stdlib's mergesort: only the permutation and, for the rows, the order are used). The corners' sort by column only shapes the tree: correctness holds for any order.
- The HashMaps of islands, split_by and the search's island numbering are modeled as association lists, which agree with them for unique ids; C17 keeps ids unique on every trace.
- Floating point in the search's cost: the tally keeps tiles, area x distance and diagonal area as integers and its total is tiles + dw * spread + gw * bent. For the shipped weights (0.5, 1.0) every term is exact, so the total is the old one bit for bit; for others it can differ from the old sum in the last place, below the search's 1e-9 tie tolerance. The differential test runs with 0.3 and 0.7 too.
- The search's own bookkeeping (cover counts and conflict bits as windows are placed and taken back, the moved list, the child loop that counts each child against the budget before bounding it, the per-depth candidate lists) is read from the code, not modeled; the differential test and the bench digests check it against place::reference.
- gather_islands with fewer than two islands returns false and changes nothing (tests/replay.rs one_island_is_left_as_it_is); the model applies gather's answer g and shows the focus whatever the island count, so it over-approximates that branch: every claim holds for any g, the refusal's included.
- arrange numbers the islands with its own flood (the sides of the windows that do not move, labels, a map to numbers) and checks the push seed with sound before the search. Islands.v proves splits, is_bridge and the search's checks from islands_fast, which partitions the same windows by the same symmetric touch, but no lemma equates the two numberings, and sound, which only decides whether the seed starts as the best, has no anchor.
- The search's tally at an interior node, where a placed window may still lie on one not yet placed, is not compared with the reference's cost_of: Cost.v ties the tile term to Rules.displaced only for layouts with no two windows overlapping (C62, C63, C68). place::tests::arrange_decides_what_the_scan_decided compares every complete placement.
- C39 proves the round trip of a zoom out and back in; that coming closer first and pulling back returns when coming closer does not scroll is not stated (C40 shows a case that scrolls and does not return).
- C17 keeps check()'s invariants on every trace except its focus-on-screen clause, which C10 proves per step.
- spot's tie on the scroll: the model compares k0 <= b0 + eps where lib.rs has key.0 < b.0 + EPS. With the model's eps = 0 on its 1/10000 px grid they agree with lib.rs's EPS of 1e-6 px; no env with eps > 0 is instantiated.
