newm-rs

        

          A Wayland compositor whose windows live on an infinite canvas of tiles, with a camera
          that pans and zooms over it. Here it runs nested in a Linux guest that runs in this tab:
          the kernel is compiled to WebAssembly, and newm-rs, waybar
          and the terminals are real aarch64 programs.
        

        

          Click the desktop to give it the keyboard. The Mod key is Alt, since the
          host keeps Super.
        

        

          
Alt = / -, Alt + wheelzoom in / out

          
Alt 0back to 1:1

          
Alt Ooverview

          
Alt + arrows or H J K Lfocus the next window; the camera follows

          
Alt Shift ← / →send a window to an edge

          
Alt Return, or + term in the bara new terminal

          
Alt Qclose a window

          
Alt Z / Alt Shift Zundo / redo

        

        

          Every terminal runs tmux and fish, with nvim as
          the editor. tmux's prefix is Ctrl A, then | or
          - splits and h j k l moves between panes.
          @help-note@
        

        

          This is the small demo, about @small-mb@ MB on the first visit. It leaves out the
          author's waybar, Neovim and Alacritty, which the
          full version (≈@full-mb@ MB) has.
        

        

          This is the full desktop, about @full-mb@ MB on the first visit. The
          small version (≈@small-mb@ MB) has newm-rs and its terminals only.
        

        
Close with Esc; ? in the corner opens this again.
